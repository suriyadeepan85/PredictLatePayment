# Independent review (QA, milestone): November 27, 2026

**Reviewer:** fresh session, repo only (`specs/agents/qa_engineer.md` section 3). Saw no build conversation.
**Scope:** commit `9919a16` (working tree clean). Test sets were not scored. Every model check below uses training-period data only.
**Method:** read the specs and code; recounted contract section 9 with my own script, without calling `load_a`/`load_b`; ran `pytest`; ran notebooks 04 to 07 headless in a scratch copy (`/tmp/qa_run`), so their side effects did not touch the repo; tried adversarial masking inputs; ran extra checks on the validation folds.

## Summary verdict

**Stage 1: PASS with conditions. Stage 2 headline: FAIL as evidence.**

- **Data pipeline:** sound and traceable. Every row count in contract section 9 reproduces exactly from independent code.
- **Leakage:** the history cut-off and the `known_by` rule are correct. No test-set row is used for training or tuning.
- **Reproducibility:** every validation number recorded in D-081, D-090, D-091, D-093, D-094 and D-095 reproduces exactly when the code is re-run.
- **Stage 2 headline:** the claim that "notes recover 85% of the ceiling's gain" (D-094) is circular. The note feature `s1_reason` equals the Disputed flag on 100% of training invoices, so Model B is in effect given the ceiling's input. The Stage 2 population also includes 801 invoices that had already been paid before the Stage 2 scoring date.
- **Before G3 or the deck:** fix or re-label these two problems, plus probability calibration (cash at risk) and the fairness rule.

## Other points a judge would challenge

- **The Stage 1 test period differs from training:** about 1% of test invoices have no history, against 11% of training invoices. Two customer numbers make up 27% of rows (D-044). Report metrics with and without them.
- **Customer-proxy features:** `payment_terms` carries 31% of XGBoost gain and `hist_late_share` 32%, so the model largely recognises customers. That is fine, but it should be said, along with how the model treats new customers.
- **Stage 2 compares across datasets:** Stage 2 is shown on IBM only (100 customers, 1,930 training invoices). Its gain should be presented with an interval (M2) and on the open-at-Stage-2 population (R-02).
- **No guard against test-set use:** nothing in `fit_predict` or `rolling_validation` stops test rows being passed in. An assertion such as `max(invoice_date) < CUT` would make "the test set is untouched" enforceable.
- **Licence:** CC BY-NC data on a publicly reachable URL is still pending Q-001.
